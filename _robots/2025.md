For the 2025 FRC season, Team 4099 competed with our robot, Peregrine. We participated in two district events, week 2 in Portsmouth, VA and week 4 in Blacksburg, VA. Unfortunately, we were unable to qualify for District Championships, but our controls lead Aanshi Patel was honored as a FIRST Dean's List Semifinalist. In the off-season, we competed at Duel on Delaware with Peregrine, and built a completely new clone robot to help with our team's knowledge transfer. We put the clone to the test at the Battle o' Baltimore event, where we finished as event finalists!
