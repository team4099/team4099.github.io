Camps

Our three camps — RoboCamps, FalconCamps, and FalconMath — are a fit for students of all ages 8+, regardless of prior experience. Lessons are led by experienced Team 4099 members. RoboCamps provides hands-on robot building for younger students; FalconCamps covers programming and 3D design for middle and high schoolers; FalconMath prepares students for high school math.

Team 4099 is a Poolesville, MD robotics team. We design, build, wire, and program a competition robot every season, and reached World Championships in 2022, winning our division. Our camps teach the same skills our members master each season — let our robots speak for themselves.

RoboCamps

  • Students ages 8-14
  • 4 sessions - Each session is 1 week, 6 hrs daily
  • 4:1 camper-to-counselor ratio
  • Learn math, engineering, and computer science skills by designing, building, and programming a robot
  • Final competition to show off what students have made
  • Guided by experienced members of Team 4099
